# Scout — research sourcing note

Session: things-to-do market research (Oct 21, 2026).

## Source quality conventions used below
- **Primary source** = the competitor's own official page, an App Store review listing, a Reddit thread, TripAdvisor listing, or the official city/tourism-board URL.
- **Published research** = peer-reviewed academic study or a published professional post (e.g., PMC, Jenny Radesky).
- Any figure or claim I could not independently verify to my satisfaction is marked **unverified** and I say why; I do not fabricate.
